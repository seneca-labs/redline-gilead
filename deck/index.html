How the models run. Three layers.

Layer 1In your tenant

Claude on Bedrock for text and reasoning. Open-weight editing models — Flux Kontext class — on your own GPUs, SageMaker or EC2.

Nothing leaves. Ever.

Layer 2An endpoint you've approved

Azure OpenAI-class image models on a private endpoint. No training by default, customer-managed keys, regional residency, ZDR available.

Contractual no-training, no-retention.

Layer 3The media egress broker

The only component permitted to reach an external endpoint. Everything else is network-denied. Next slide, in full.

One door out, audited.

Premium editing runs either on models inside your environment, or through an endpoint you've already approved. Either way, one audited service sends the cropped region and the instruction. Never the submission.

One door out.

When an edit uses the approved external endpoint, the media egress broker is the only path. Here is everything it enforces, on every request.

Payload minimizationThe mask region plus padding, never the whole asset. Flattened raster, EXIF and XMP stripped, no layers, no filenames.
Context strippingPixels and a short instruction only. Never PRC comments, reviewer identities, claims language, part numbers, brand or product names.
Outbound DLPOCR runs before egress. If ISI, claims, or identifiers appear in the crop, it doesn't leave.
Ephemeral by constructionPer-request credentials, in-memory only, nothing at rest.
Audited end to endRequester, asset, region, endpoint, ZDR flag, timestamp, input and output hashes.
